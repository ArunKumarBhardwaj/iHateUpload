## Method and blind spots

**Corpus.** 20 invented documents (names, numbers, addresses; example.com URLs only) made by `gen.ts`: Chrome HTML+CSS (invoice, bank statement, research paper, CV, form, slides, letter, newsletter, receipt, chart report, LaTeX-like, RTL, CJK, spreadsheet, contract, brochure), pdf-lib (AcroForm, annotations, memo drawn with standard fonts) and rasterised JPEG pages with skew (scan). `latex-cff` sets Source Serif 4 (OFL, OTF/CFF outlines, in `assets/`) through Chrome. No real or personal PDFs are used. Truth is written by the generator.

**Run.** Both sides through the browser runner (`run.mjs`, Playwright + Chrome): ours from a local `next dev` on :43127, ihatepdf.cv with every option on, one conversion per document, sequential, 1.5 s pause between rival conversions. "iHP Exact" is their Exact layout mode.

**Structural score.** The existing scorer (`../score.ts`) plus: CJK and RTL documents score text by character F1 (no word spaces; extraction order varies) and skip the order score; the annotations document adds a `comments` score read from word/comments.xml.

**Visual score.** Original PDF and converted .docx are both turned into PDF pages (the .docx via LibreOffice 26.8 headless, `soffice --convert-to pdf`), rasterised with macOS PDFKit (`pdf2png.swift`, 600 px wide), greyscaled, converted to an ink map, downscaled to 200 and 100 px wide, blurred, and compared by SSIM over 8x8 windows where either page has ink (`visual.ts`). Page score is the mean of the two scales; document score is the mean over the original's pages (a missing page scores 0). It rewards ink in the right place and density; it is not perceptual.

**Blind spots.** Treat visual scores as relative, not absolute: values are low for any editable (re-flowed) output because text lines shift by a few pixels. LibreOffice is not Word: it handles w:cols, shading, text boxes and font fallback differently (our CJK output names a Latin font and renders blank here, theirs renders boxes, so the `cjk` visual score is unreliable; fonts missing on this Mac are substituted for every side). Page-size mismatches are stretched to the original's aspect. Tiny pages (receipt) and text-light pages are noisy. One run per document, so there is no variance estimate. Vector shapes are not scored structurally. The `forms`, `footnotes`, `comments` and `links` features rest on one document each.
